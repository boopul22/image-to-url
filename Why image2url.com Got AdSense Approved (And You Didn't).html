⊕ THE PLUS BUTTON & POPUP AD — What Is It?

This is Google AdSense "Auto Ads" with Multiple Formats

The ⊕ plus button at the bottom-right and the popup that opens when you click it are NOT custom-built features by your competitor. They are automatic Google AdSense ad formats that Google places on the page after approval. Here's how it works:

Your competitor only added ONE single script tag to their site:

<script src="adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-XXXXX" crossorigin></script>

Google's "Auto Ads" system then automatically scans the page and places multiple ad formats in optimal positions. That's why you see 10 ad units, 8 auto-placed, and 9 Google iframes — all from one script. The ⊕ plus button, the "Discover more" related topics, the banner at the bottom, the in-content display ads — all are automatically placed by Google after approval.

All the Ad Formats Google Auto-Placed on Their Site:

1 ⊕ Plus Button (Bottom-Right Expandable) AUTO

The small pill-shaped button at the bottom-right (e.g., "⊕ Graphic design tools") is a Related Search for Content (RSC) ad unit. When clicked, it opens a popup showing an ad "based on your other interests." This is a Google-controlled ad format — your competitor did NOT build this. Google places it automatically after AdSense approval when "Auto Ads" is enabled.

2 "Discover More" Related Topics Section AUTO

The section showing related topic links like "Image editing software", "Photo storage solution", "Share images easily" — this is another AdSense Related Search ad format. Each link opens a Google search results page with ads. Again, placed automatically by Google, not built by the competitor.

3 Anchor/Sticky Banner Ad (Bottom) AUTO

The display banner stuck to the bottom of the viewport (Adobe Creative Cloud ad, etc.) is an Anchor ad. It has a collapse arrow (⌄) to minimize. Also placed automatically by Google Auto Ads.

4 In-Content Display Ads (Multiple) AUTO

Display ads injected between content sections (between testimonials, below comparison table, etc.) — 8 auto-placed ad slots throughout the page. Google's algorithm decides where to insert them based on page content flow.

5 Vignette/Popup Ad on Click AUTO

The full-screen popup ad that appears when you first interact with the page — this is a Vignette ad. It shows between page loads or on first interaction. Another automatic Google placement.

⚡ Key Takeaway

You don't need to build ANY of these ad features yourself. Once Google approves your AdSense account and you enable "Auto Ads," Google will automatically place all of these formats on your site. The real question is: what did your competitor do to get APPROVED in the first place? That's what we need to focus on below.
